A · Fresh Confirm

FRIENDLY · CLEAR · TRUSTWORTHY

Built around the green tick that means "booked". Feels like a calm, reliable tool any salon owner or clinic manager trusts on day one. The closest to modern software brands like Linear or Cal.com, but warmer.

Logo concept

A calendar tile with a tick inside. "yes" is heavy, "book" is lighter, so the name reads as the promise. Works as a phone app icon and a favicon.

Colour palette

Confirm Green#0F8A5F
Deep Pine#0B2A22
Mint (dark mode)#34D399
Coral spark#FF7A59
Mist#F3FAF6
Night#0D1412

Gradients go green to mint with a soft coral glow. Coral is used sparingly, mostly for the "Book a free demo" button and highlights.

Typography

Booked solid.
Online and in-store booking, client records, reminders and an AI receptionist, in one app with your name on it.
Headings: Bricolage Grotesque (bold, a little quirky) · Text: Inter (very readable on phones)

Icon style

Rounded 2px line icons with a soft tinted fill. Friendly and easy to read at small sizes.

B · Salon Luxe

ELEGANT · WARM · PREMIUM

Speaks the language of beauty and wellness first. Deep plum with blush and a soft gold, plus an elegant serif. It makes the apps you build feel like a premium upgrade for a salon or spa, while staying clean enough for clinics and tutors.

Logo concept

A "Y" monogram that also reads as an open pair of scissors or a ribbon, with a gold pin where the strokes meet. Italic "book" adds a boutique touch.

Colour palette

Aubergine#3D1736
Orchid#8C2F6B
Blush#F2C6D2
Soft Gold#D9B779
Pearl#FBF5F7
Velvet night#1A0C18

Gradients blend orchid into blush, like a soft studio light. Gold is only for small accents (icons, dividers, the logo pin), never for text.

Typography

Every chair, beautifully booked.
Online and in-store booking, client records, reminders and an AI receptionist, in one app with your name on it.
Headings: Instrument Serif (elegant, editorial) · Text: Manrope (modern, rounded, readable)

Icon style

Fine 1.4px line icons with one small gold dot each. Light and refined, like a spa menu.

C · Bold Signal

CONFIDENT · TECH-FORWARD · AI

Leads with the AI phone agent. A speech bubble that says "yes" is the logo, so the brand itself feels like a receptionist answering. Electric cobalt and a sunshine highlight stand out next to competitors and fit yesbook.ai well.

Logo concept

"yes" lives inside a chat bubble, the answer every caller wants to hear. The bubble alone becomes the app icon and favicon.

Colour palette

Signal Cobalt#2F4BFF
Midnight#0A0F2E
Sunshine#FFE14D
Periwinkle (dark)#7C8CFF
Ice#F4F6FF
Deep space#060918

Cobalt carries the brand, sunshine marks the single most important thing on a screen (the call to action, the "yes"). Gradients stay blue to periwinkle, no purple.

Typography

Never miss a call.
Online and in-store booking, client records, reminders and an AI receptionist, in one app with your name on it.
Headings: Unbounded (wide, bold, techy) · Text: DM Sans (clean and friendly)

Icon style

Solid, chunky filled icons on coloured tiles. Bold and instantly readable, like app icons.
