See what we build

Online booking

A booking page on your own domain that looks like your brand. Clients pick a service, a stylist and a time, then get an instant confirmation.

  • Real-time availability
  • Deposits and cancellation rules
  • Add to Apple or Google Calendar
  • Share on Instagram, Google and WhatsApp

In-store booking

Your team sees the whole day at a glance on a tablet or computer. Drag to move, tap to book, and never double-book a chair again.

  • Day, week and staff views
  • Walk-in queue
  • Colour-coded services
  • Works on iPad, laptop and phone

Client records

Remember the colour formula, the allergy, the favourite stylist. Every visit is saved so your team can give a personal welcome.

  • Visit history and spending
  • Private notes and photos
  • Birthdays and preferences
  • Import your existing list

Staff and services

Set each person's hours, breaks and the services they offer. Prices and durations can be different for each staff member.

  • Staff hours and time off
  • Service menu with add-ons
  • Per-staff pricing
  • Roles and permissions

Reminders

Clients get a confirmation, a reminder before their visit and an easy way to reschedule. Fewer empty chairs, less phone tag.

  • SMS and email reminders
  • One-tap confirm or reschedule
  • Rebooking nudges
  • Your wording, your brand

Reports

Simple, clear reports that answer real questions: who are my best clients, which services sell, and when should I add staff?

  • Daily and monthly revenue
  • Staff performance
  • Top services and clients
  • Export to Excel

Automatic secure backups

Encrypted daily backups mean your bookings and client records are safe, even if a device is lost or something goes wrong.

  • Daily encrypted backups
  • Secure sign-in for staff
  • Restore when you need it
  • Your data stays yours

AI phone agent (premium)

When you're busy with a client or closed for the night, the AI agent picks up, answers questions and books straight into your calendar.

  • Answers 24/7
  • Books, moves and cancels
  • Natural, friendly voice
  • Sends you a summary of every call
