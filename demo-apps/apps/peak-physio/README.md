# Peak Physio & Rehab (physio clinic)

A clickable sample booking app built by Yesbook. It is a made-up business used to show clients what their own app can look like.

Open `index.html` in any web browser. No install or internet setup is needed (fonts load from Google Fonts when online).

- **Client app**: home, booking flow, premium AI assistant chat, My visits.
- **Owner app** (switch at the top): dashboard, calendar, clients, services and prices, team, packages and offers, AI assistant settings, reports and settings.

Bookings made in the client app show up in the owner app, and price, package and service changes made by the owner show up in the client app straight away. Everything stays in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
